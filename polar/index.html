Postal history is more than a station location

An official postal cancellation invalidates postage, while an expedition or souvenir cachet normally does not. A cachet alone is not evidence of postal entry at a mapped site. Entries without source-reviewed postal details are marked not assessed; this does not prove that no polar mail exists. Flags indicate operators or associated postal administrations, not uncontested territorial sovereignty. An institute website is not an invitation to send unsolicited mail. Temperature requests are sent directly from the visitor’s browser to Open-Meteo. Embedded webcam views may connect to the station operator’s server, where normal web-request information such as the visitor’s IP address is processed.
