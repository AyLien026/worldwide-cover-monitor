What makes a station philatelically interesting?

Station postmarks, expedition cachets and official mail routes can document scientific history. But a research station is not necessarily a public post office, and its official website is not an invitation to send unsolicited mail. Each entry links to the station operator and searches publicly available Worldwide Covers articles; it does not claim a cover or an authorised mail-in service exists.
