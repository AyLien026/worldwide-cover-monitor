Browse international letter rates by country or postal territory. The usual rate to Germany is shown first; expand any entry for other destination zones, services and official sources.

Most rates are for ordinary international letters to Germany. Entries explicitly labeled Europe or Rest of World, as well as Germany's worldwide rate, use the stated destination zone. Tariffs depend on the destination, size, weight and service; many collector-supplied figures remain unverified.

Verification notice. An automated source scan tests whether a linked postal website is accessible and still contains the expected price text. "Source reviewed" records a past manual inspection, not a guarantee of ongoing validity.
